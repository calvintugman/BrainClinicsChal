## 1. Level versus shape (definitions)
Vector x = the 12 log absolute powers (3 ROIs frontal/central/posterior x delta/theta/alpha/beta; natural log of uV^2; v2 pipeline; ordering as in the earlier mapping: ROI-major, bands delta,theta,alpha,beta), separately for EC and EO.
level = mean(x) (a physiological+measurement mixture; NOT labelled artifact). shape = x - mean(x) ("centered log-power shape"; NOT conventional relative band power; shape elements sum to 0, rank 11).
MISSING-DATA RULE (fixed now): a vector is COMPLETE only if all 12 elements are finite; if any element is missing the vector's level and all 12 shape elements are NaN (no centering on available elements). Condition-level missingness propagates: EO-minus-EC contrast and deviation are NaN if EC or EO vector is incomplete; levels of a complete condition stay available. In outcome models NaN is median-imputed inside treatment-training folds only (no missingness indicators).

(2a) SHAPE MAPPING: ridge EC shape -> EO shape. Compared with (a) the reference-training mean EO shape and (b) persistence: EC shape + reference-training mean (EO shape - EC shape). Metric: held-out squared error in raw log-power units, per person averaged over the 12 elements; paired differences between predictors with 95% bootstrap intervals over evaluation people (2000 draws, rng seed 1), plus per-element MSE.

(2c) DEVIATION RELIABILITY: for the evaluation people, recompute (v2 cleaning, same windows) per-window ROI band POWERS in uV^2 (before logs), split each recording at the midpoint of its retained clean time span into disjoint portions (half 1 = windows ending at or before the midpoint; half 2 = windows starting at or after it; no shared samples); average linear powers over the windows of a half, THEN take ln, then form level/shape per half and deviation = EO shape - fixed-mapping prediction from EC shape of the same half (the mapping fitted on reference-training only; deviations divided by the cross-fitted reference-training residual SD per element). ## 3. NUMERICAL OUTCOME-BLIND FEASIBILITY CRITERIA (fixed before evaluating 2a-2c)
F1 (mapping improvement; GATING): the shape ridge's aggregate held-out MSE is lower than that of BOTH (a) and (b), the paired bootstrap interval of the MSE difference excludes 0 against each, AND the relative aggregate MSE reduction versus the stronger of (a),(b) is >= 5%. Rationale: the deviation is observed-minus-predicted EO shape; if the learned mapping does not beat trivial persistence-with-mean-shift, the deviation is indistinguishable from the simple contrast already in model C; 5% is a modest practical floor (smaller gains would leave the deviation almost equal to persistence residual plus noise).
F3 (deviation reliability; GATING): N >= 100 eligible evaluation people AND median over the 12 elements of the SB-corrected split-half correlation >= 0.50 AND at least 8 of 12 elements have a bootstrap lower bound >= 0.30. Rationale: with a reliability below 0.5 the maximum observable correlation of a feature with any outcome is below sqrt(0.5) ~ 0.71 of its true value and noise dominates n=164 models; 0.30 lower bounds guard against a few elements carrying the median.
F2 (INFORMATIONAL, not gating): whether the uncentered ridge beats amplitude-only (paired interval excluding 0, relative reduction >= 5%); used only to interpret the earlier R2 0.805 (shared amplitude vs structure).
COVERAGE (INFORMATIONAL, not gating): fraction of patients' EC/EO levels and shape elements inside the reference-training 1st-99th percentile range; standardized mean differences; interpretation rule: any element with <80% coverage is flagged as a limitation.
IF F1 OR F3 FAILS: STOP; no remission scoring; no criteria revision, no feature selection, no alternative mapping.
